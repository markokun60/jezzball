Welcome to Jezzball

Jezzball is a highly addictive game for Windows 
that was originally designed by a Microsoft 
in 1992 as part of the Microsoft Entertainment Pack. 

Your  objective is to claim at least 75% of the game area, 
while avoiding balls with the lines that you create. 

A line will expand horizontally or vertically until it hits 
a wall and becomes a boundary. 

If a ball touches a line before it becomes a boundary, 
the line is destroyed and you lose a life.
